Egg Fried Rice

Serves 4 · Prep 10 min · Cook 15 min · Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked rice, preferably chilled from the day before
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Scramble the eggs until just set, then move them to a plate.
  2. Add the remaining oil and the frozen vegetables. Cook 4 minutes.
  3. Add the rice and break up any clumps. Cook 4 to 5 minutes, until hot.
  4. Stir in the soy sauce, sesame oil if using, the eggs, and the green onions. Serve warm.
