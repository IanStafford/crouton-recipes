Chicken Rice Bowl

Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup long-grain white rice
  • 1 lb boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 cup shredded carrots
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey

Steps

  1. Cook the rice according to the package directions. Keep warm.
  2. Season the chicken with garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  3. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken 6 to 8 minutes, until browned and cooked through.
  4. Stir together the soy sauce and honey. Add to the skillet and toss to coat.
  5. Divide the rice among four bowls. Top with chicken, cucumber, and carrots.
