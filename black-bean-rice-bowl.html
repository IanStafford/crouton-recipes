Black Bean Rice Bowl

Serves 4 · Prep 10 min · Cook 20 min · Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1 cup long-grain white rice
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 cup frozen corn
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup salsa
  • 1 avocado, sliced (optional)

Steps

  1. Cook the rice according to the package directions.
  2. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the corn and cook 3 to 4 minutes.
  3. Add the black beans, cumin, chili powder, and salt. Cook 5 minutes, stirring often.
  4. Divide the rice among four bowls. Top with the bean and corn mixture, salsa, and avocado if using.
